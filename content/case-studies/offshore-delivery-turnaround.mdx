I selected and hired every one of its engineers myself. They worked on the company's own accounts and licences, in its own cloud and delivery tooling, on the same squads and to the same standards as Sydney. A local employer of record provided employment and HR, not delivery. Everything operational ran through me: devices, identity and access, security, tooling, project management, standards and delivery.

<Section name="lessons">

> Bad fixes at that level are a governance problem, not a testing problem.

One thing I got wrong was a hire, and it changed my process. I selected a senior hire on background and a coding test, and did not weight behaviour anywhere near enough. Afterwards, behaviour became an explicit assessment criterion rather than an impression, and I widened the panel beyond myself, bringing in peers and, for lead roles, the engineers who would report to the hire.
